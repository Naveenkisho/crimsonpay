Frequently asked questions

Is this a live payment service?

This is a preview build for testing. It doesn't process real money, cards or crypto — balances, addresses and card numbers are generated in your browser and nothing is sent to any server. Please don't enter real passwords or ID numbers.

Which cryptocurrencies are supported?

BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT and USDC across networks like TRC20, ERC20, BEP20, Bitcoin and Solana.

How do I order a card?

Create an account, choose the $10 virtual card or $100 physical card, then continue to the secure wallet payment flow. No identity check is required before payment.

What are the fees?

$10 virtual / $100 physical issuance, $0 monthly, 1% top-up, 1.2% FX, 2% ATM (min $2), free deposits, and per-network withdrawal fees. The full schedule is on the Fees page inside the app.

Where would the card be accepted?

A real card of this type works anywhere major card networks are accepted — this build simulates purchases with a "Simulate spend" button instead.

Can I reset my data?

Yes — Settings → About → "Reset data" restores everything to the initial state.

Before you go live (read this)

  • You need a licensed card issuer / BIN sponsor — you can't issue cards from a website.
  • Crypto→fiat makes you a VASP/MSB: registration, KYC/AML, sanctions screening, monitoring.
  • PCI-DSS, custody, a liquidity provider and banking/settlement partners.
  • India/INR: RBI does not treat crypto as payment; FEMA + PMLA + 1% TDS apply. Real players issue USD/EUR cards via offshore entities.
  • "0% fee" while hiding a large spread is treated as deceptive in many markets — disclose the effective rate.

Frequently asked

My deposit isn't showing up

Deposits credit after network confirmation — in this preview, ~5 seconds after you press "Simulate incoming deposit".

My card payment was declined

Common causes: the card is frozen, the amount exceeds your daily limit, or the card balance is too low. Check Cards → Manage.

How do I raise my limits?

Complete identity verification in Settings → Verification, then adjust per-card limits from the card's Limits button.

How do refunds work?

Merchant refunds return to the card balance automatically, usually in 3–5 business days (instant in this preview).

Can I use the card abroad?

Yes — non-USD payments convert at the live rate plus a 1.2% FX fee.
