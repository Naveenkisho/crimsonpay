PARTNER FRAMEWORK

Partnership & Collaboration Agreement

Baseline terms for proposed issuer, wallet, merchant, technology, marketing, and distribution collaborations. A signed order form is required before any partnership is active.

Effective 1 August 2026Version 1.0Detailed policy
These pages explain platform rules and safeguards. They do not claim that CrimsonPay, Trust Wallet, or any named payment network has a partnership, licence, or endorsement unless a separate written announcement expressly confirms it.

1. No automatic partnership

A proposal, integration discussion, compatibility statement, or platform use does not create a partnership, agency, joint venture, endorsement, or authority to bind another party. Collaboration begins only after authorised representatives sign a separate agreement.

2. Scope and order forms

A signed order form should define parties, territory, services, technical duties, milestones, service levels, commercial terms, support ownership, launch approval, and duration.

3. Due diligence and compliance

Each party maintains required licences, registrations, policies, sanctions controls, privacy safeguards, and consumer disclosures for its role and completes reasonable security, ownership, financial, and regulatory diligence.

4. Brand and communications

Names, logos, screenshots, ads, press releases, and public statements require prior written approval from the relevant owner. Compatibility must not imply endorsement. Approved assets must follow current guidelines.

5. Confidentiality and data

Non-public product, security, commercial, customer, and technical information is protected and used only for the collaboration. Personal-data roles, purpose limits, subprocessors, retention, and transfer safeguards must be documented.

6. Security and integration duties

Each party protects credentials, endpoints, systems, and personnel access using least privilege, authentication, logging, monitoring, incident procedures, and timely vulnerability remediation.

7. Commercial records

Revenue share, fees, taxes, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, reserves, invoicing, audit rights, and reporting apply only as written in a signed order form.

8. Changes, suspension, and termination

Material production changes require approval. A party may suspend activity for urgent security, sanctions, legal, or fraud concerns. Termination must address transition, data return or deletion, payments, confidentiality, and brand removal.

9. Liability and disputes

Each party remains responsible for its representations, people, systems, compliance, and misconduct. Warranties, indemnities, caps, insurance, governing law, and dispute procedures apply only as agreed in writing.
