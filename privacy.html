Privacy Policy

How CrimsonPay handles personal information, wallet connection data, device information, support records, and card-service details.

Effective 1 August 2026Version 1.0Detailed policy
These pages explain platform rules and safeguards. They do not claim that CrimsonPay, Trust Wallet, or any named payment network has a partnership, licence, or endorsement unless a separate written announcement expressly confirms it.

1. Scope and responsibility

This policy applies when you visit the website, request a card, connect a supported wallet, contact support, or manage card services. The entity named in your service confirmation is responsible for the processing described here.

2. Information collected

Information you provide

  • Cardholder name, country, mobile number, email, delivery information, card preferences, selected limits, and support messages.
  • Verification information required by an expressly identified authorised issuer or provider.

Information generated through use

  • Public wallet addresses, requested networks, transaction references, signing status, and connection metadata.
  • Device, browser, security events, cookies, and page interaction records.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Information supports requested services, fraud prevention, limit enforcement, legal compliance, reliability, support, and material service notices. Processing may rely on contract, legal obligation, legitimate security interests, or consent depending on location. We do not ask for or store recovery phrases or private keys.

4. Sharing and international transfers

Necessary information may be shared with an identified issuer, regulated payment provider, identity-verification provider, infrastructure host, fraud service, adviser, or authority. Cross-border transfers use safeguards required by applicable law.

5. Retention and security

Records are retained only for operational, dispute, fraud-prevention, tax, and regulatory periods, then deleted or de-identified. Safeguards include encrypted transport, access controls, monitoring, and restricted operational access.

6. Rights and choices

Subject to local law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection and may complain to a competent authority. Essential records may be retained where law or fraud prevention requires it.

7. Cookies, children, and third parties

Essential storage supports security and preferences. Optional analytics should operate only where enabled. Services are not directed to children. Wallets and third-party sites maintain separate privacy practices.

8. Changes and contact

Material changes are dated on this page. Privacy requests should use the official support channel displayed in the product.
